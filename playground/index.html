Renew

A small language for Canadian mortgage renewals

Language guideSourceReference

Your term ends. Rates moved. Write the decision as a short program: the mortgage, the choices, and the checks you care about. Renew runs it here, shows every formula, and compiles it to JavaScript you can keep.

The language in one screen

      

        
Blocks
mortgage "Home"
  balance $412,500
  rate 4.79% fixed
  compounded semi-annually
  amortization 20 years
  payments monthly
  term 5 years

scenario "Shorter" from "Home"
  rate posted 3 year on 2026-11-18
  term 3 years
A scenario copies the mortgage above it and changes only what you write.

        
Statements
let budget = $3,000
compare all by payment, interest, payoff
show payment of "Shorter" - payment of "Home"
show schedule "Home" first 12
show yearly "Home"
explain "Home"
check payment of "Shorter" <= budget
show rate needed for "Home" at budget
show amortization needed for "Home" at budget

        
Units are types
$ money, % rates, years and months lengths of time, plain numbers, and dates. $400,000 * 1% is $4,000.00. $5 * $5 is an error, and so is a rate written without a percent sign.
Measures
payment, yearly, interest, principal, balance, total, rate, payoff, three months interest. Each is read as measure of "Plan".
Real rates
posted 1 year, 3 year or 5 year on DATE reads the Bank of Canada weekly series for a conventional mortgage offered by chartered banks, January 2016 to November 18, 2026. These are posted rates, not offers.
